# Trade Wars - Feature TODO

Based on Trade Wars 2002, these features are missing from the current baseline.

## High Priority (core gameplay feel)

- [ ] **Autopilot** — type a destination sector, computer plots shortest path and auto-warps through each hop (1 turn each). Pauses if pirates encountered mid-route.
- [ ] **Sector fighters** — drop your fighters in a sector to defend it. They attack anyone entering. Retrievable when you return.
- [ ] **Ferrengi NPC ships** — roaming enemy ships that move through the galaxy hunting you, steal cargo on contact, destroyable for big bounty.
- [ ] **Multi-sector macro movement** — type a chain of sector numbers (e.g. `5 12 34 49`) at the move prompt to warp through them in sequence.

## Combat & Weapons

- [ ] **Port attacking** — attack a port to loot credits/stock. Ports have fighter defenses. Attacking Fed-space ports triggers a wanted status.
- [ ] **Photon torpedoes** — heavy one-shot weapon bought at Stardock, bypasses some shields.
- [ ] **Armid mines** — lay mines in sectors that auto-damage enemies entering them.
- [ ] **Escape pod** — one-use item that saves 1 hold of cargo on ship destruction. Must be rebought at Stardock.

## Planet System

- [ ] **Colonist transport** — pick up colonists (uses hold space), deliver to your planets to boost production speed.
- [ ] **Planet citadels** — build a fortress on your planet using deposited ore/equipment. Has its own shields and fighters.
- [ ] **Genesis torpedoes** — expensive Stardock item. Creates a new planet in an empty sector, or destroys an existing one.

## Economy

- [ ] **Port leveling** — ports gain XP per trade and level up (1–10), improving prices and stock capacity over time.
- [ ] **Corp bank / interest** — deposit credits at Fed HQ or Stardock, earn small interest per turn. Credits on your ship are lost on death without an escape pod.
